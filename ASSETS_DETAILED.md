## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Images
- Use PNG for graphics, JPG for photos
- Keep image size under 5MB (usually much smaller)
- Images display in a 600x600px box, so square aspect ratio is best
- Make sure they're funny and match the tone!

### For Videos
- MP4 with H.264 codec is most compatible
- Keep under 50MB for quick loading
- 30 seconds or less is ideal
- You can test videos locally first

### For Audio
- MP3 is most compatible
- Mono is fine (don't need stereo)
- Keep 1-3 seconds for best effect
- Can download off YouTube using online tools
