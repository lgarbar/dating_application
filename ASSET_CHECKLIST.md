## 🔍 File Naming - IMPORTANT!

**Exact names required** (case-sensitive):
```
✅ CORRECT:
- escalation_1.png
- 6-7_meme_edit.mp4
- escalation_1.mp3

❌ WRONG:
- Escalation_1.png (capital E)
- escalation 1.png (space instead of underscore)
- 6-7_meme_edit.MP4 (uppercase extension)
- escalation_01.mp3 (_01 instead of _1)
```

## 📞 Tips

**Images**
- PNG or JPG works fine
- Keep under 5MB each
- Square aspect ratio is best (600x600px)
- Funnier is better!

**Videos**
- MP4 is best (most compatible)
- Keep under 50MB
- 30 seconds or less
- Test playback before using

**Audio**
- MP3 is best
- Keep 1-3 seconds
- Should be funny/punchy
- Test volume level
